Lauren Crumley Personal Stylist

A private, single-user styling assistant. It reads one person's own Pinterest boards (her wardrobe inventory and her inspiration board) so it can help her plan outfits and decide what to buy.

It is not a public app, has no other users, and isn't available for download or sign-up. It only reads. It never posts, saves, edits, deletes, or comments on Pinterest.

This project is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Pinterest.
