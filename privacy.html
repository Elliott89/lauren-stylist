Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Who this covers: This app is used by one person, Lauren Crumley, on her own Pinterest account. No one else uses it.

What it accesses: With her permission, it reads the pins and boards on her Pinterest account, including her private boards. It requests read-only access.

What it does with it: The content is used only to help her with outfit and shopping decisions. It is processed on a private computer in her home. Her pin content may be sent to an AI model provider only to generate styling suggestions for her.

What it never does: It never posts, edits, or deletes anything on Pinterest. It never sells, shares, or publishes her data, and never uses it for advertising.

Storage: Data stays on her private home computer. Nothing is stored on a public server.

Your control: She can revoke access at any time in her Pinterest settings, and she can ask for any stored data to be deleted.
